# Recruitment Market

The recruitment market starts as a catalog section, not a separate transaction
platform.

The first useful objects are:

- `talent`: one installable capability package.
- `agent-template`: a reusable virtual member profile with recommended Talents.
- `role-posting`: a reusable owner need that can be fulfilled by a virtual
  member template or custom Talent bundle.

This keeps hiring-style flows on the same manifest, permission, install-plan,
health, and audit rails as ordinary Talents.

## Not In Scope Yet

- Payments or revenue share.
- Third-party identity verification.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Live marketplace search service.
- Automated execution of third-party code.

Those should come after signing, review, rollback, and tenant isolation policies
are mature.
